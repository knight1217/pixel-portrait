FAQ

Is SnapShift free?

Yes — during our Free Beta, all templates are completely free.

What photos can I use?

Portraits, pets, landscapes, objects, products — almost anything. Clear images work best.

What is 2K quality?

2K doubles the output resolution for sharper, print-ready images. 1K is fine for social media.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

Photos are only used for generation and are not stored or shared.

Can I use the images commercially?

Yes — for personal and commercial use, as long as you own the original photo.

Will pricing change?

SnapShift is free during Beta. In the future, some premium features may have a small cost.
