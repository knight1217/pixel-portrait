Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use SnapShift, you may upload photos for AI image generation. We do not require account creation or personal information to use the service.

2. How We Use Your Photos

Photos you upload are sent to our AI image generation provider (Agnes AI) to create the requested images. We do not store, sell, or share your uploaded photos. Generated images are returned to you and are not retained on our servers.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We may use basic analytics and cookies to understand website usage and improve our service. These do not identify you personally.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare for website hosting and security, and Agnes AI for image generation. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

SnapShift is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@snapshit.fun.
