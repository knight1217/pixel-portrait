Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

1. What We Collect

We do NOT require sign-up. When you use Pixel Portrait:

Uploaded photos are transmitted to our AI image generation partner for style conversion. Neither we nor our AI partner retain your photos after the image is generated. Generated images are hosted temporarily and may be deleted periodically.

We use third-party AI image generation APIs to create your portraits. These services receive only the image data needed for processing and do not receive your name or contact details.
